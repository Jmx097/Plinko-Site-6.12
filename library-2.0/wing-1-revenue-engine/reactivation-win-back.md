# Reactivation & Win-Back

> **One-line promise:** a trigger-driven second life for every lead you already paid for — stalled conversations, lost deals, and parked tiers, re-approached when something actually changes.

| | |
|---|---|
| **OWNER** | You; the signal watcher feeds it |
| **TRIGGER** | Event-driven (a signal fires on a known contact) + a quarterly sweep |
| **THE NUMBER IT MOVES** | Meetings booked from existing list (the cheapest meetings you'll ever get) |
| **BELT** | Amber |

Your list is an asset with a second harvest. Everyone in it already cost you sourcing, scoring, and sends. The old way to reactivate was "it's been 90 days, checking in" — which is a cold email with extra guilt. The upgrade: **reactivate on triggers, not timers.**

## The pools (who's eligible)

| Pool | Who | Re-approach posture |
|---|---|---|
| **Stalled positives** | Replied positive, went quiet after the 3-touch spine | Warmest. Reference the old thread directly. |
| **Closed-lost** | Real conversation, clear no, 90+ days ago | "Things change" — lead with what changed on *their* side. |
| **Objection graveyard** | Pushed back with a reason (timing, budget, incumbent) | Re-open ONLY when the trigger answers their stated objection. |
| **Parked B-tier** | Scored B at the gate, never worked | Promote to A when a P1 signal fires on them. |

## The System

1. **Tag the exit reason at the moment of exit.** "Closed-lost: timing" and "closed-lost: has incumbent" are different re-entry doors. If you don't tag it now, win-back later means re-reading every thread.
2. **Watch your pools for the same P1 signals you source with** — new leadership hire, relevant job posting, funding/acquisition, public post in your problem space. A signal firing on someone who already knows you outranks the same signal on a stranger.
3. **Match the trigger to the stated objection.** "Budget" + they just raised = door open. "Bad timing" + new ops hire = door open. "Happy with incumbent" + nothing changed = stay out; a timer is not a trigger.
4. **Open with the change, not the history.** One line on what changed, one line on why it's relevant, one low-friction ask. Never open with "circling back" or an apology for the gap — you're not resuming a pitch, you're starting a better-timed one.
5. **Quarterly sweep for the untriggered.** Once a quarter, pull stalled positives and closed-lost older than 90 days with no signal activity, and send one honest break-up-grade note ("closing the file unless...") — it costs nothing and reliably shakes out a few real answers.
6. **Cap it at one reactivation attempt per trigger.** A trigger buys you one touch, not a new 5-touch sequence. If they engage, the normal reply spine takes over; if not, back to the pool.
7. **Track reactivated bookings separately** from cold bookings. This number is the proof that list hygiene and exit-tagging pay — and it's usually the best cost-per-meeting line on your scoreboard.

## Agents Attached

The signal watcher (Wing 3) monitors the pools. Reply triage handles whatever comes back. The quarterly sweep is a 30-minute human job — calendar it.

## Copy This

The **win-back opener skeleton**: `Hey {{first_name}}, saw [the change] at {{company}}. When we talked in [month] the sticking point was [their stated reason] — [the change] usually shifts exactly that. Worth a fresh look?` Three sentences, no "just checking in," no apology.
