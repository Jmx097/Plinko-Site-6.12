## Your dot

Every member is a dot on the same chart: people adopting AI in their business, somewhere between "haven't started" and "runs a full agent system." The placement quiz you took on the way in put you on that chart. Your dot color is your belt:

| Belt | You are... | You unlock |
|---|---|---|
| **Grey** | Curious, nothing shipped yet | Wing 0 (Start Here) + The Vault |
| **Green** | First automation shipped and running | Same wings, plus the 30-Day course in full |
| **Amber** | 3+ automations live, documented time reclaimed | The Revenue Engine + The Delivery Engine |
| **Red** | Runs a full agent system with governance | The Agent Workforce + The Scoreboard |

Belts map to your Skool level. You move up by shipping, not by watching. The community feed is where you post the proof.

## Your first 7 days

1. **Day 0:** Finish the Campus Orientation course ("Find Your Dot"). It takes about an hour and ends with your personal ROI target and a baseline of your current numbers.
2. **Day 1:** Post your dot in the community: where you are, the one time-leak you're killing first.
3. **Day 1–2:** Start Module 1 of "Your AI Digital Employee in 30 Days."
4. **This week:** Attend the live strategy call (recordings posted if you miss it).
5. **Day 7:** Ship the first automation, however small. Post it. That's your Green Belt.
